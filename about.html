Built for local businesses

LocalCart gives small businesses and independent makers a place to sell online without building a website of their own, and gives shoppers one place to discover and support them.

Who we are

LocalCart is a multi-vendor marketplace built exclusively for South African small businesses and independent makers. We believe that every local entrepreneur — whether they're baking sourdough in their home kitchen, weaving baskets in Khayelitsha, or making skincare in Oudtshoorn — deserves a professional online presence that works as hard as they do.

We bring together vendors from across the country under one digital roof, so shoppers can discover, browse, and buy from multiple local businesses in a single checkout. No hopping between Instagram DMs. No WhatsApp orders that get lost in the chat. Just one cart, every vendor.

Why we do it

South Africa's small business sector is vibrant, creative, and resilient — but it's also fragmented. Most independent vendors sell informally through social media, word of mouth, or market stalls. They often spend more time chasing orders and answering payment questions than actually making their products.

Shoppers, meanwhile, want to support local but often don't know where to start. They might stumble on a great vendor on Instagram, but then have to remember to check their story for drops, DM them to order, and figure out payment separately — and that's just for one item. Multiply that across a few vendors, and the friction becomes too high.

LocalCart solves this by giving vendors a ready-to-use online storefront with proper cart, checkout, and order tracking — and giving shoppers a single destination where they can intentionally support local makers, all in one place.

Our purpose in the community

We're not just building an app — we're building an ecosystem. Every Rand spent on LocalCart goes directly to a small South African business, helping to keep money within local communities, create jobs, and sustain the creative and culinary traditions that make this country special.

For vendors, we offer a professional storefront and order management system that would otherwise cost thousands of Rands to build and maintain. For shoppers, we make it easy to vote with their wallets for the kind of economy they want to see: one where small businesses can thrive alongside big retail, and where local makers are celebrated, not overlooked.

LocalCart is proudly South African — built for our people, by people who believe in the power of local commerce.
